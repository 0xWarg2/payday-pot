# PayDay Pot — Build-in-Public Visual Style

Use this style for every day-by-day illustration in the Season 4 build log.

## Fixed visual language

- Landscape 16:9 composition for X.
- Warm off-white sketchbook paper with subtle natural grain.
- Imperfect black fineliner, graphite shading, loose construction lines, rough arrows and small correction marks.
- Lime highlighter for saving, principal and PayDay Pot product ideas.
- Electric cyan marker for encryption, onchain activity, draws and prizes.
- One main visual idea per image, supported by a few small doodles.
- Sparse handwritten English labels; use no more than five short phrases.
- Product-design notebook energy, not a polished advertisement or corporate infographic.

## Recurring branding

- `PAYDAY POT` is the primary handwritten title.
- Add the official Zama wordmark only as a small secondary signature with `built with` or `Season 4 build` context.
- Never combine Zama into the project name or imply that PayDay Pot is an official Zama product.
- Keep the Zama signature visually subordinate to the PayDay Pot title.

## Content rules

- Draw only features that exist or are currently being explored that day.
- Show encrypted values as dots, hatching or masked marks, never as fake plaintext amounts.
- Keep employer-funded prizes visually separate from employee principal.
- If withdrawal is shown, make clear that principal returns to the saver.
- Do not imply wallet-address anonymity; the product protects amounts, balances, TWAB and winnings.
- Avoid generic crypto imagery, dense code, glossy 3D, neon backgrounds and excessive padlocks.
